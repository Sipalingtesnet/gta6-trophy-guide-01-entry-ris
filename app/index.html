Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough. Nothing needs a second run: there is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep saves rather than restarting.

  1. Phase 1 Story — 60 to 80 hours

    Play the main story from start to finish without detouring. Twelve story trophies unlock on their own along the way, and everything you touch during missions counts toward the 100% checklist later, so you are never wasting time. Keep the manual saves described below as you go from chapter to chapter.

  2. Phase 2 100% Completion — about 100 hours

    The checklist only appears after the credits, but it already tracks what you did in Phase 1. Work category by category instead of chasing the percentage: vehicles, animals, weapons, stunts, challenges, bank vaults, illegal operations and meditation spots. This phase is where most of the activity, collectible, crime and exploration trophies fall, and it is also the longest single stretch of the platinum.

  3. Phase 3 Strangers & Freaks and cleanup — about 10 hours

    Mop up the side-story strands, any partner trophies you skipped, and the stragglers your filters show as unearned. Free roam is fully open after the story, so nothing here is timed. The platinum lands as soon as the 54th trophy does.

Before you start

  • One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so a missed trigger means a fresh run.
  • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and one more right before the final mission.
  • Cheats disable trophies. Do not enter any, even once, on the save you intend to finish.
  • No trophy needs the online modes, so the whole list is solo work.
  • Free roam stays open after the story, which is when the cleanup phases happen.

Missable trophies

One trophy on the list can be lost for good.

Positive Mental Attitude

This is the only trophy on the list that can be missed. It comes from the balcony meditation in Chapter 1, and the chapter moves on whether or not you took the moment. Do it as soon as the balcony is available, and keep the Chapter 1 manual save so you can step back if you are unsure it registered. Every other trophy can be picked up at any point, during the story or in free roam afterwards.

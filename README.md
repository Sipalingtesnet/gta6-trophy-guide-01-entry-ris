[![Rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-trophy-guide-01/Sipalingtesnet/gta6-trophy-guide-01-entry-ris/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)
[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-trophy-guide-01/Sipalingtesnet/gta6-trophy-guide-01-entry-ris/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)

An offline web app that is the best guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI — built for the [make.fun bounty gta6-trophy-guide-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01).

A checklist saved in the browser, export/import, filters by grade and kind, a spoiler setting for story trophies, a roadmap to the platinum, missable flags, and a guide in our own words for every trophy with sources cited.
